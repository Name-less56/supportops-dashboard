LEGAL / STORAGE

Cookie policy

SupportOps uses one strictly necessary authentication cookie and one local preference to remember your privacy choice.

Effective: November 6, 2026Version 2.0

1. What these technologies do

A cookie is sent with requests to the same site. Local storage remains in the browser and is not automatically sent to the server. SupportOps does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. Storage inventory

NamePurposeCategoryRetention
supportops_sessionAuthenticates the signed-in user and applies role/group access.Strictly necessary; HttpOnly; SameSite=StrictUp to 8 hours or until sign-out.
supportops-consent-v1Remembers the privacy-banner choice.Necessary local preferenceUntil site storage is cleared.
supportops-preferences-v2Remembers the theme, density and motion settings you choose, so they apply on every page.Functional local preference, written only when you change a settingUntil you reset preferences or clear site storage.

3. Consent controls

The authentication cookie is necessary for the requested sign-in service and cannot be disabled while using the dashboard. No optional analytics or marketing storage is set. The banner provides equally clear choices and can be reopened from “Cookie settings” in the footer.

4. Managing storage

Use “Sign out” to invalidate the server-side session. Browser settings can remove cookies and local storage. Removing the session cookie signs the user out; removing the preference makes the banner appear again.

5. Changes

This inventory must be reviewed whenever a new SDK, analytics service, embedded resource or browser-storage key is introduced.
