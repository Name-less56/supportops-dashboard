Privacy policy

This notice explains how the SupportOps demonstration handles authentication, support tickets and privacy choices.

Effective: November 6, 2026Version 2.0
Production readiness notice. This is a portfolio demonstration. Before deployment, the operator must add its legal identity, privacy contact, hosting location, actual processors and jurisdiction-specific rights, and obtain qualified legal review.

1. Scope and controller

The person or organisation deploying SupportOps is responsible for the database and becomes the controller of information entered into it. The bundled records and accounts are fictional and intended only for evaluation.

2. Information handled

SupportOps stores a username, display name, one-way password hash, role, support group, account state and security timestamps. Tickets may contain a subject, description, category, group, channel, priority, status, tags, comments and operational timestamps. Customer profiles, conversations, knowledge articles and satisfaction metrics are synthetic. The demonstration does not require real customer names, telephone numbers, payment data, precise location or sensitive information.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Account data is used to authenticate users and enforce role-based access. Ticket data is used to demonstrate queue management and operational analytics. A production operator must document its applicable legal basis before processing real personal data.

4. Storage and disclosure

Application data is stored in the PostgreSQL instance configured by the operator. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; session and reset tokens are stored as hashes. This build contains no advertising, behavioural analytics, third-party browser SDKs, external fonts or tracking pixels. CSV exports are created on the user’s device and remain under their control.

5. Retention

Authentication sessions expire after eight hours and password-reset links after twenty minutes. Demo accounts and ticket records remain until an authorised administrator changes or deletes them, or the database is reset. A production deployment must establish its own retention schedule.

6. Security and access

SupportOps uses HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict session cookies, role and group scoping, hashed credentials, parameterised database queries and a restrictive Content Security Policy. These safeguards reduce risk but do not replace secure hosting, TLS, backups, monitoring and an incident-response process.

7. Your choices and rights

You can reject optional browser preferences from the cookie banner, sign out to end the active session, and ask the deploying operator to access, correct or delete information where applicable. Do not enter real personal data into this demonstration.

8. Changes

The effective date and version will change when this notice is materially updated.
